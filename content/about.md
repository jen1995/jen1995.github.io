Hi! I'm Eugenia Elistratova — I love mathematics, machine learning and spontaneous shenanigans. **Cohomology Zero** is a blog where I write about machine learning and about math for its own sake.

And hi, I'm [Ivan Petrov](https://github.com/ipetr0v) — I practice safe programming, collect cat memes, and nerd out fearlessly :) I joined the blog for the quantum road, starting with the post on the Dirac delta.

The name comes from [«Группы и теория гомотопий (трэш трейлер)»](https://youtu.be/mqAf5lOJZew) — a legendary Russian-language trash trailer for a homotopy theory course. We unapologetically nerd out about math here, patiently waiting for the environment to answer: cohomology — zero?..

Current and past series:

- **The quantum road** (in progress, with Ivan) — from a black box with an arrow to the hydrogen atom: a minimal but rigorous path through quantum mechanics, with a notebook at every station.
- **The Fourier road** — from a guitar string to the spectrogram, then the four shades of Fourier and the sampling theorem.
- **Transformers from scratch** — a step-by-step walk from RNNs with attention to the full Transformer architecture.
- **Generative models** — VAEs and friends, with the math worked out carefully.
